WHO WE ARE

More than just food.

MStore started in 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic. Our family decided to start the business because we needed a way to support ourselves during one of the most difficult times in our lives. Before establishing MStore, my husband and I were self-employed and supplied ingredients, including fish and seafood, to Japanese-Chinese restaurants such as Kamayan, Saisaki, and Dad's. This was our main source of income, which we relied on to cover our family's daily expenses. Unfortunately, our family faced several challenges when the pandemic began. Our other source of income, hog breeding, came to an end when African swine fever struck. These events made our financial situation even more difficult. Despite these hardships, my family did not give up. In the middle of the crisis, we established MStore to earn a living and support our family's needs. Through hard work, determination, and perseverance, MStore became an important source of income. Thanks to the business, we were able to overcome our financial struggles and even renovate and improve our home. Today, MStore represents more than just a business to us. It symbolizes our family's resilience, unity, and determination to overcome life's challenges. Our journey reminds us that even in the most difficult situations, there is always hope and an opportunity to start again. Through MStore, we have been able to offer affordable food to people who need a filling meal to stay nourished and energized throughout the day.

We believe that food is more than something we eat. It is a way to connect with friends, celebrate moments, and create memories. Our goal is to make every customer feel welcome with every order.

From our crispy lumpia to our sweet Filipino desserts, we want every visit to be enjoyable, convenient, and worth coming back for.
